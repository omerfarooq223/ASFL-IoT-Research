## Review Highlights & Addressed Feedback

The final revision incorporates feedback from the peer-review process:
1. **Evaluation Scope & Scalability:** In response to reviewer suggestions regarding scale beyond 10-node setups, the evaluation framework was extended to evaluate sensitivity across 25 and 50 clients, 50 communication rounds, and a wider multilayer perceptron (384 hidden units) under active 10% packet loss and 10% Byzantine sign-flip attacks.
2. **Precision & Framing:** Clarified metric definitions (False Negative Rate corresponding to adversary admission under trust warming, and False Positive Rate corresponding to benign node rejection).
